About

Artist name: Richa Tejas

Richa Tejas is an Indian contemporary representational artist known for her vibrant exploration of color and landscape. Working primarily in oil and mixed media, her practice centers around the expressive power of hues — a philosophy reflected in her artistic identity, Happily Hued.

Her work captures the beauty and simplicity of nature through layered skies, luminous horizons, and richly textured surfaces. Rather than dramatizing the landscape, Richa highlights its quiet strength — the subtle shifts of light, atmosphere, and tone that often go unnoticed.

She has participated in curated exhibitions and has also established a presence within the NFT art space, expanding her practice into digital platforms while maintaining the integrity of her painterly style.

Artist statement

Color is the starting point of my practice.

I am deeply inspired by the ever-changing hues of nature — the way a sky transforms within minutes, how light softens a landscape, or how layers of color create emotional depth. Through representational landscapes and inspirational subjects, I aim to preserve these fleeting moments of beauty and stillness.

My process is intuitive yet deliberate. I build each painting through careful layering, allowing tones to interact and evolve until they achieve harmony. I am particularly drawn to subtle transitions between colors — the spaces where one hue gently dissolves into another — because they mirror the emotional gradients we experience in life.

At its core, my work is about appreciation. In a fast-moving world, I hope my paintings invite viewers to pause, observe, and reconnect with the simple, uplifting presence of color and nature.
